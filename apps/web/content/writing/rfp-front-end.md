What I learned writing front-end scopes for 100+ enterprise engagements over 16 years at TandemSeven: a good estimate is less about confidence and more about naming where uncertainty lives.

The mistake I still make is assuming everyone shares the same definition of done. They rarely do. The scope has to make the invisible work visible before the project starts.
